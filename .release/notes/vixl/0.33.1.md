# Vixl 0.33.1

## Changes

Lottie export keeps source easing and linked-instance timing.

### Changed

- **Lottie keeps easing.** Each span between keyframes becomes one Bezier key when the source curve (linear,
  `ease-*`, `cubic-bezier()`, cut where another track's key falls) reproduces every sampled frame. Keyframes
  between export frames get their own fractional key times. Layers turn and scale about their box centre, and
  position splits into x and y when the axes ease differently. Other spans (bounce, elastic, spring, steps,
  colour mixing, combined tracks) keep per-frame held keys, counted in the result's new `motion.baked` with a
  warning (#177).
- **Lottie keeps linked-instance timing.** `link` and `animation-instance` layers become precompositions of their
  own vectors, played by a time remap, instead of image sequences. Offset, speed, trim, loop and ping-pong are
  straight runs of the remap, hidden edges set in and out points, and crops become masks. Identical instances share
  one composition and `motion.instances` counts them. Animated link placement and corner pins fall back to images
  with a reason (#177).
