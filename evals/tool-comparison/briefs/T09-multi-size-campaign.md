# T09 · One campaign, six sizes

A launch campaign for a café's winter menu, delivered in six sizes that clearly belong together.

**Brand.** Tidewick Café is a small, fictional harbor-side café. Colors: navy `#14263b`, lamp amber
`#f2a541`, sea-foam `#a8d5c8`, coral `#e2725b`, cream `#f7f1e5`. Warm, coastal, unfussy.

**Copy.**

```
The Winter Menu is here
Gingerbread flat white · Smoked maple latte · Cardamom bun
From Tuesday, December 1 at Tidewick Café
See the menu at tidewick.example
```

The headline is "The Winter Menu is here". The two smallest sizes may drop the second and fourth
lines; every other size uses all four lines exactly.
