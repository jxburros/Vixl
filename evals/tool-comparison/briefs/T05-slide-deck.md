# T05 · Slide deck

A short, good-looking deck for a café staff meeting. 16:9.

**Brand.** Tidewick Café is a small, fictional harbor-side café. Colors: navy `#14263b`, lamp amber
`#f2a541`, sea-foam `#a8d5c8`, coral `#e2725b`, cream `#f7f1e5`.

**Slides.** Six slides, in this order, with this content:

1. **Title.** "Tidewick Café — 2025 in Review". Subtitle: "Staff meeting · January 2026".
2. **By the numbers.** Three big statistics: "35,280 cups poured", "312 open-mic performers",
   "4.8★ average review".
3. **Cups by quarter.** A bar chart: Q1 8,560 · Q2 8,830 · Q3 8,990 · Q4 8,900. Label each bar with its value.
4. **What worked.** Bullets: "Thursday open mics doubled evening sales" · "Cold brew on tap from April
   to September" · "A pre-order pickup shelf shortened morning queues".
5. **2026 priorities.** Bullets: "A second espresso machine for the weekend rush" · "A monthly makers'
   market on the quay" · "Launch the winter menu on December 1".
6. **Thank you.** "Thank you — see you on the quay."

Write 2–3 sentences of **speaker notes** for every slide. Give the deck one consistent look: the
same title position, type sizes and footer on every content slide, and a footer with the café name
and the slide number.
