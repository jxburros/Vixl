# T03 · Print poster

A poster for a winter night market, to be sent to a commercial printer.

**Copy.** Use exactly this text:

```
Port Ellery Quay
Harbor Lights
A winter night market on the water
Saturday, December 5, 2026 · 4–10 pm
Lantern parade at 6 pm
Forty local makers
Hot cider & roasted chestnuts
Live brass band
Free entry · All ages · portellery.example
Presented by Tidewick Café
```

"Harbor Lights" is the title and "Port Ellery Quay" the kicker above it. The four lines from
"Lantern parade" to "Live brass band" are highlights; present them as a list.

**Imagery.** Paper lanterns strung above dark harbor water, with their reflections. The mood is festive
but calm. Colors are up to you.

## Print spec

- Trim size: **11 × 17 in** (US Tabloid), portrait.
- Bleed: **0.125 in** on every side. The background and any edge-to-edge artwork must extend into it.
- Keep all text at least **0.25 in inside the trim**.
- Raster content at **300 dpi** or more at final size.
- The printer wants **CMYK**.
