# T01 · A picture

Make an illustration of **a lighthouse on a rocky point at dusk**.

- The sky is a smooth gradient from deep navy at the top to warm amber at the horizon.
- The lighthouse stands on rocks on the **left** half of the picture. Its beam sweeps out to the **right**, across the sea.
- **Exactly two** small sailboats are on the water.
- At least five small stars are in the upper sky.
- Style: flat, layered, like a modern travel poster. Clean shapes, no outlines, no photographic texture.
- **No text, letters or logos anywhere.**

| File | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| `picture.png` | 2400 × 1600 px, RGB |
| your editable source | if your tool has one (a project file, SVG, HTML, script …); otherwise skip |
