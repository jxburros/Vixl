# T16 · Café menu (text-heavy print)

A one-page menu to print on a desktop printer.

**Page.** US Legal, 8.5 × 14 in, portrait, margins of at least 0.5 in, no bleed. Title "Tidewick
Café"; under it "Coffee by the harbor". Brand colors if you want them: navy `#14263b`, amber
`#f2a541`, cream `#f7f1e5`.

or another clear way to connect names to prices; no text smaller than 9 pt at print size; everything
on one page.
