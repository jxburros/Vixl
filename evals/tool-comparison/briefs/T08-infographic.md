# T08 · Infographic from data

An infographic built from `evals/tool-comparison/fixtures/coffee-2025.csv`: cups sold per month in
2025 at a fictional café, by drink (`flat_white`, `drip`, `cold_brew`, `tea`).

**Content.**

1. Title "A Year of Coffee at Tidewick"; subtitle "Cups sold per month, 2025".
2. A **stacked bar chart**: one bar per month, stacked by drink, with a legend and a value axis that
   starts at zero.
3. The **share of the year** by drink, in any suitable chart, with percentages to one decimal place.
4. Three **callouts**, computed from the data: total cups in 2025; the busiest month and its total;
   the month cold brew peaked and how many cups it sold.
5. A footnote: "Fictional data, made for testing."

Use the same color for each drink everywhere. Every number shown must be correct and every bar
must be drawn to scale. Brand colors if you want them: navy `#14263b`, amber `#f2a541`, sea-foam
`#a8d5c8`, coral `#e2725b`, cream `#f7f1e5`.
