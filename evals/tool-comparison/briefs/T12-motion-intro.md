# T12 · Animated intro

A 6-second animated logo intro for a café's social video.

**Brand.** Tidewick Café is a small, fictional harbor-side café. Colors: navy `#14263b`, lamp amber
`#f2a541`, sea-foam `#a8d5c8`, cream `#f7f1e5`.

**Timing.** Square, 1080 × 1080, 30 fps, navy background.

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.5 s | Three layered waves draw in across the bottom third, left to right |
| 1.0–3.0 s | A simple lighthouse rises into view; from 2.0 s its amber beam sweeps back and forth, and keeps sweeping to the end |
| 2.0–3.5 s | The wordmark "Tidewick Café" slides up and fades in above the waves |
| 3.0–4.5 s | The tagline "Coffee by the harbor" types on, one letter at a time |
| 4.5–6.0 s | Hold; everything visible, beam still moving |

Movements ease in and out; nothing jumps.
