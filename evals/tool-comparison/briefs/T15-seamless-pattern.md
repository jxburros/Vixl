# T15 · Seamless pattern

A repeating pattern for café merchandise.

**Motifs.** Kelp fronds, small spiral shells, curling waves and scattered dots, in navy `#14263b`,
amber `#f2a541`, sea-foam `#a8d5c8` and coral `#e2725b` on cream `#f7f1e5`. Spread them evenly but
not in a visible grid, with varied sizes and rotations.

**Seamless.** The tile must repeat with no visible seams: anything crossing an edge continues exactly
on the opposite edge.
