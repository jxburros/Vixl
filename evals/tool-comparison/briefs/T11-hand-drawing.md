# T11 · From hand drawing to clean art

Turn a phone photo of a pen sketch into clean, colored artwork that keeps the drawing's character.
The sketch is `evals/tool-comparison/fixtures/sketch.jpg` unless you were given a different photo.

**Clean-up.**

- Remove the paper, its shadows and the desk; correct the tilt.
- Straighten lines that were clearly meant to be straight and make the circle round.
- Close small gaps where lines were meant to meet.
- Keep everything else as drawn. Don't redraw the picture, add objects or change the composition.

**Color.** Fill the closed shapes with flat colors under the lines: roof coral `#e2725b`, house walls
cream `#f7f1e5`, door and window panes navy `#14263b`, sun amber `#f2a541`, tree canopy green
`#5a8f5a`, trunk brown `#7a5236`, the background above the ground line pale blue `#d9ecf2`, and
below it sea-foam `#a8d5c8`.

## Deliverables

| File | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| `sketch-clean.png` | Black lines on white, 2000 px wide |
| `sketch-lines.svg` | The lines as editable vector strokes or paths (no embedded raster images) |
| `sketch-color.png` | The colored artwork, 2000 px wide |
| `compare.png` | The original photo and your colored result side by side |
| your editable source | if your tool has one |

## When you're done

Put every file in the run folder you were given. Then write `REPORT.md` there, listing each file
and how you made it, anything in this brief you didn't do or did differently and why, and anything
you're unsure about. Be exact: the report is checked against the files.
