# Tidewick Café — 2025 in Review

Staff meeting · January 2026

Notes:
Welcome, everyone, and thank you for coming in before opening. This morning we look back at 2025 in a few numbers, celebrate what worked, and agree on our three priorities for 2026. It should take about fifteen minutes, with time for questions at the end.

## By the numbers

- 35,280 cups poured
- 312 open-mic performers
- 4.8★ average review

Notes:
Here is our year in three numbers. We poured 35,280 cups, welcomed 312 performers to the open-mic stage, and held a 4.8-star average across our reviews. Every one of those numbers is the work of the people in this room.

## Cups by quarter

```chart bar
Quarter,Cups
Q1,8560
```

Notes:
Cups grew steadily through the year, from 8,560 in the first quarter to a peak of 8,990 in the summer. The fourth quarter dipped only slightly, to 8,900, even with fewer cold-brew sales. Steady quarters like these let us plan staffing with confidence.

## What worked

- Thursday open mics doubled evening sales
- Cold brew on tap from April to September
- A pre-order pickup shelf shortened morning queues

Notes:
Three things made the biggest difference this year. Thursday open mics turned a quiet evening into one of our busiest, and cold brew on tap carried us through the summer. The pre-order shelf took pressure off the morning rush, so thank you for keeping it tidy and labelled.

## 2026 priorities

- A second espresso machine for the weekend rush
- A monthly makers' market on the quay
- Launch the winter menu on December 1

Notes:
For 2026 we have three priorities. A second espresso machine should cut weekend waits, and a monthly makers' market will bring new faces along the quay. We will launch the winter menu on December 1, so menu ideas are welcome from now on.

## Thank you — see you on the quay.

Notes:
Thank you all for a wonderful 2025. Questions and ideas are welcome now, or drop them in the notebook behind the counter. See you on the quay.
