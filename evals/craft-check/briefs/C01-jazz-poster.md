# C01 · Jazz night poster

A poster for a small harbour-side jazz night, to print at A3 and post online.

**Size:** 3508 × 4961 px (A3 at 300 dpi). **File:** `poster.png`.

**Copy, exactly:**
- Lowtide Jazz Night
- The Mara Quinn Trio
- Friday 14 November · Doors 7:30 pm
- The Boathouse, 2 Harbour Steps, Port Ellery
- Tickets £12 at lowtide.example

The look is up to you. It should feel late-night, warm and a little smoky, and read from across a room.
