# Licensing: who can use Vixl for free

[Documentation home](../README.md) · [Getting started](../getting-started.md) · [Licence text](../../LICENSE)

Vixl is licensed under the [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](../../LICENSE). This page explains that licence in
plain words so you can tell whether it covers you before you install. It is a summary, not legal advice, and it
changes nothing: where this page and the licence text differ, the licence text is what counts.

## Is Vixl open source?

No. Vixl is source-available: the source is public and you may read it, change it and pass it on within the licence,
but the licence limits who may use it, which open-source licences do not.

## Who can use it for free

- **Individuals.**
- **Companies under both small-business limits.** Use for the benefit of your company is permitted when the company
  - has fewer than 100 people working for it, counting employees and independent contractors together, **and**
  - had less than US$1,000,000 total revenue in the prior tax year.

Both limits must be met; meeting one is not enough.

Two details of the licence text matter when you count:

- **The revenue limit is in 2019 dollars.** The licence adjusts it for inflation with the United States Bureau of
  Labor Statistics' consumer price index for all urban consumers (U.S. city average, all items, not seasonally
  adjusted), so the figure in today's dollars is higher than 1,000,000.
- **"Your company" is wider than one legal entity.** It is any legal entity, sole proprietorship or other kind of
  organization you work for, together with the organizations that control it, that it controls, or that are under
  common control with it. Count the people and the revenue of that whole group.

## Who needs a commercial licence

A company that is over either limit. The licence's notice says it directly: larger companies need a separate
commercial licence from the licensor. To ask about one, open an issue or contact
[@jxburros](https://github.com/jxburros) on GitHub.

## Passing Vixl on

Anyone who gets a copy of any part of Vixl from you must also get the licence terms (or their URL) and the lines in
[`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) that begin with `Required Notice:`. The licence does not let you sublicense or transfer
your own licence to someone else.

## Where the licence is stated

- The repository's [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) file, which the Python package ships as its licence file. The package
  metadata names it `LicenseRef-PolyForm-Small-Business-1.0.0`.
- The Claude Desktop bundle (`vixl.mcpb`) and the Claude Code bundle carry the same file, and the bundle manifest
  names the same identifier.
