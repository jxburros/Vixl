# Vixl — Ripple logo kit

The Vixl wordmark: **v** and **i** stand above a waterline at half the x-height; **x** and **l** are their
own reflection. The x is a v over its mirror image, and the l's lower half is the reflection of its
stem. Below the waterline the reflection breaks into seven bands that drift sideways, like a
reflection on choppy water. The symbol is the x on its own.

Every file here is generated by `build.py` (run `python assets/brand/ripple/build.py` from the
repository root). Change the geometry or colours there, rebuild, and commit the outputs; do not edit the
exports by hand. The exploration that led to this logo is in `explorations/logo-alternatives/`
(concepts T1 and T7).

## Choose a layout

| File name starts with | Best use |
| --- | --- |
| horizontal- | The wordmark: website headers, presentations, documents, signatures |
| mark- | The symbol: compact placements, watermarks, avatars |
| app-icon-dark | App icons and dark avatars; square, opaque charcoal background |
| social-avatar-light | Light social avatars; square, opaque white background |
| favicon-light / favicon-dark | Browser icons on light / dark backgrounds |
| horizontal-…-animated | The wordmark with its ripple moving: hero sections, intros, loading states |

There is no stacked lockup: the wordmark already carries the symbol in its x.

## Choose a color version

- `color`: charcoal letters, blue ripple fading to pale blue. Use on white or light backgrounds.
- `reverse`: white letters, light-blue ripple fading towards the background. Use on dark backgrounds.
- `black`: solid black, ripple included. Use for one-color printing and light surfaces.
- `white`: solid white. Use for one-color marks on dark surfaces. Transparent white files can look blank
  in a white preview.

## Choose a format

- **SVG:** scalable vectors for websites and design software. Every letter is a path; no font is needed.
- **PNG:** transparent core logos at 1x and 2x. App and social tiles are opaque.
- **PDF:** vector core logos for print (RGB; a printer can convert to their profile).
- **ICO:** browser favicons with 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px images.
- **GIF / WebP / MP4:** the animated wordmark, a seamless 1.6 s loop on an opaque background (white for
  color, charcoal for reverse). Use the WebP or MP4 on the web; the GIF where nothing else plays.
- **Vixl:** editable masters in `Editable-Vixl/`, one path layer per letter and per ripple band. The
  animated masters keep the motion as ordinary keyframe tracks.

## Sizes included

- Wordmark: 1200 × 500 and 2400 × 1000 PNGs. Symbol: 800 × 800 and 1600 × 1600 PNGs.
- App and social tiles: 1024 × 1024 master, plus 48, 96, 180, 192, 256 and 512 px PNGs and a JPG.
- Favicons: 512 px master, plus 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px PNGs and a multi-size ICO.

Seven ripple bands blur together at small sizes, so the favicons and the 48 and 96 px app and social
icons use a simplified symbol with three thicker bands. Use the detailed symbol from 128 px up.

## Brand details

- Charcoal `#252B39` (letters), blue `#3575EE`, mid blue `#5B8FF5`, sky `#6A9AFF` and pale `#A9C4FF`
  (the ripple, top to bottom), white `#FFFFFF`.
- The letters are drawn geometry, not a font. Inter Tight 800 and Inter remain the brand typefaces for
  text set next to the logo.

## Use consistently

Keep the proportions, the waterline and the band offsets intact; do not redraw the ripple or straighten
the bands. Leave at least the height of the i's dot clear around the visible logo. Keep the wordmark at
least 160 px wide on screen and the detailed symbol at least 128 px; use the favicon artwork below that.
Use the square app artwork without pre-rounded corners; the platform applies its own mask.

## Folder contents

- `SVG/`, `PNG/`, `PDF/`: core logos (wordmark and symbol, four colour versions each) and icon artwork.
- `Icons/`: app and social PNGs and JPGs, favicon PNGs and ICOs.
- `Animated/`: the moving wordmark as GIF, WebP and MP4.
- `Editable-Vixl/`: editable masters.
- `00-kit-overview.png`: quick visual reference.
- `build.py`: the generator for all of the above.
