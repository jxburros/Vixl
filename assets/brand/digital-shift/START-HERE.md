# Vixl — Digital Shift logo kit

The selected paired-V logo, prepared in multiple layouts and color treatments. All versions retain the opposite-facing, slightly offset V shapes and pixelated edges.

## Choose a layout

| File name starts with | Best use |
| --- | --- |

| mark- | Standalone symbol, compact branding, watermarks |

| social-avatar-light | Light social avatars; square, opaque white background |

| favicon-dark | Small browser icons on dark backgrounds |

## Choose a color version

- `color`: charcoal upper V and wordmark with blue lower V. Use on white or light backgrounds.
- `reverse`: white upper V and wordmark with lighter blue lower V. Use on dark backgrounds.
- `black`: solid black. Use for one-color printing and light surfaces.
- `white`: solid white. Use for one-color marks on dark surfaces. Transparent white files can look blank in a white preview.

The color, reverse, black and white treatments are available in horizontal, stacked and symbol-only layouts: 12 core versions.

## Choose a format

- **SVG:** scalable vectors for websites and design software. The lettering is outlined, so you do not need to install a font to display the exported logo.
- **PNG:** ready to use in slides, documents and image uploads. Core logo PNGs have transparent backgrounds. App and social tiles have opaque backgrounds.
- **PDF:** vector core logos for print layouts and sharing with a printer. These are RGB artwork; a printer can convert to their required color profile.
- **ICO:** browser favicon containing 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px sizes.
- **Vixl:** editable masters with separate V shapes, pixels and editable text.
- **JPG:** opaque app and social tiles where an upload requires JPEG.

## Sizes included

- Horizontal: 1200 × 500 and 2400 × 1000 PNGs.
- Stacked: 800 × 900 and 1600 × 1800 PNGs.
- Symbol: 800 × 800 and 1600 × 1600 PNGs.
- App/social tiles: 1024 × 1024 master, plus 48, 96, 180, 192, 256 and 512 px PNGs.
- Favicons: 512 px master, plus 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px PNGs and a multi-size ICO.

The favicon masters omit the two smallest loose pixels on each side to improve small-size clarity. Use the detailed symbol at larger sizes.

## Use consistently

Keep the logo's proportions, offset and pixel spacing intact. Leave at least the width of one large detached pixel clear around the visible logo. Use SVG when possible; otherwise choose a PNG at least as large as its displayed dimensions. As a starting point, keep the detailed horizontal logo at least 200 px wide, the stacked logo at least 140 px wide and the detailed symbol at least 48 px wide. Use the simplified favicon below that size and check the result at its actual display size.

Use the square app artwork without pre-rounded corners; the destination platform can apply its own mask. The avatar has padding for common circular crops.
